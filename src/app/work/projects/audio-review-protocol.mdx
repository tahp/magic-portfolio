## Overview

The Audio Review Protocol (ARP) was created to address the lack of consistency in audio gear reviews. Instead of relying on "vibes" or subjective impressions, ARP provides a structured, 10-step sequence that ensures every pair of headphones or earbuds is tested against the same rigorous standards.

## Key Features

- **Standardized Testing**: A guided protocol covering 10 essential dimensions, including Fit & Comfort, Sound Quality, ANC, Transparency, and Latency.
- **Repeatable Workflow**: Ensures that reviewers follow the same procedure every time, making it possible to compare different products on a level playing field.
- **Detailed Logging**: Allows reviewers to track specific product details and log detailed observations for each test phase.
- **Progress Tracking**: A built-in checklist keeps the reviewer on track through the comprehensive testing sequence.

## Technologies Used

- **Next.js**: For building a fast, responsive user interface.
- **Vercel**: For seamless deployment and hosting.
